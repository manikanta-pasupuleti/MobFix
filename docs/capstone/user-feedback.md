# MobFix User Feedback Plan

## Purpose

User feedback should verify whether the workflow is understandable to customers
and efficient for staff. It should not be used to manufacture positive results;
responses and limitations should be reported as collected.

## Participants

- Three to five people who have booked a device repair
- One administrator or repair-shop operator
- One technician or technically experienced participant

## Tasks

1. Find a screen-repair service.
2. Register or sign in.
3. Choose an appointment slot.
4. Submit a repair request.
5. Find the booking in the customer dashboard.
6. Approve a sample quote.
7. As staff, assign the booking and update its status.

## Measures

| Measure | Collection method | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Task completion | Observer checklist | At least 80 percent of participants complete each core task |
| Time on task | Stopwatch or screen recording | Booking completed within 5 minutes by most participants |
| Error count | Observer notes | No more than two avoidable errors per core task |
| Confidence | Five-point questionnaire | Average score of at least 4 for status clarity |
| Qualitative feedback | Short interview | Record recurring confusion and requested improvements |

## Reporting format

Report participant profile, task results, direct observations, recurring themes,
and changes made after feedback. Do not publish names, contact details, or
recordings without consent. Results are pending until the study is conducted.
